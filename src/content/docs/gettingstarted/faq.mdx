---
title: Frequently Asked Questions
---

- **Q: How can I install Elysium Days?**
  - **A:** Refer to the Modpack Installation section in the wiki!

---

- **Q: Where can I find the server files for Elysium Days?**
  - **A:** There are no specific files for servers. However, you can learn how to get the ZIP version of the modpack from the [Server Installation](https://elysium-days-wiki.netlify.app/howtoinstall/serverinstallation/) section.

---

- **Q: Will Elysium Days be ported to other versions?**
  - **A:** No, only 1.21.1 is supported at the moment.

---

- **Q: Is Elysium Days gonna be available on CurseForge?**
  - **A:** Elysium Days will never be available on CurseForge due to various reasons.

---

- **Q: What's the hardware requirements to run Elysium Days?**
  - **A:** If your PC can run Minecraft on vanilla properly, and has a spare 6144 MB that you can allocate to Elysium Days, you can run it. If you don't know how to allocate more RAM, check [Memory Allocation](https://elysium-days-wiki.netlify.app/guides/memoryallocation/) section of the wiki.

---

- **Q: I added a mod & I clicked update all & I enabled the disabled mods = now the modpack is not opening.**
  - **A:** I do not support any modifications to the modpack, if something is disabled it's for a reason, same goes for the mods that are not updated, it's completely intentional if something is the way it is.

---

- **Q: Does Elysium Days work with OptiFine?**
  - **A:** No, OptiFine will never be supported. Elysium Days uses completely different set of mods and a loader.
