## Nether

To reach the Nether you must find a ruined portal then use the Dimensional Tear Bottle to activate a portal. Flint and Steel will no longer work to open portals.

## End

To reach the End you must find 12 different eyes, you can't use the same eye twice. They're obtained through exploring and their tooltips contains hints on where to find them. If you want to ruin all the fun you can check [this wiki](https://www.minecraft-guides.com/mod/end-remastered/).

After you have done so, you need to find the Lost Castle, which you can do by buying the Lost Castle map from a cartographer. It might take a couple of tries.
